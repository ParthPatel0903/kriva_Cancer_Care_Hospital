About Kriva Cancer Care Hospital

We deliver world-class oncology care with compassion, expertise and advanced medical technology. Our multidisciplinary team builds a treatment plan around each patient's specific condition — not a one-size-fits-all protocol.

Beyond medicine, we focus on emotional well-being, nutritional guidance and continuous support, so every patient and family feels informed, comfortable and cared for at each stage of treatment.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted cancer care hospitals by delivering innovative treatment with compassion and excellence.

Our Mission

Personalised treatment, early diagnosis, advanced technology and patient-centred care, delivered with consistency.

Surgical Oncology

Radiation Oncology
